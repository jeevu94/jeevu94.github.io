Our Story

From India to the world

House of UNNIVA is a premium fashion and lifestyle apparel brand born from the rich heritage of Indian craftsmanship and reimagined for the global stage. Every piece is a dialogue between tradition and modernity — woven with intention, tailored with precision, and designed to transcend borders.

We believe luxury is not loud. It is felt. It lives in the weight of a fabric, the cut of a silhouette, and the quiet confidence of those who wear it. From India to the world, we craft apparel that speaks a universal language of elegance.
